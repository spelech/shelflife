Standardized filter dropdowns to match uniform styling

Replaced multiple native filter dropdowns (Type, Status, Vote, and Sort) in the Media Grid and Community Grid with a new custom UI `Select` component. These customized dropdowns now feature full dark mode support across all browsers and correctly highlight the active sorting/filter selection in the accent brand color, making it easier to tell what filters are currently active at a glance.
